# Derussification source (thread 03)

`derussification.json` (and the generated `src/lib/ukrainian-frequency/derussification.data.mjs`
plus the public `public/downloads/ukrainian-frequency/derussification/lemmas.csv`)
is a DERIVED artifact, not a source corpus.

## What it is

The thread-01 frequency list joined against the open Ukrainian idiomatic-style
tooling. For each of the top-12,000 lemmas the tooling would replace, it records
the native alternative the tooling recommends, that alternative's rank on the
frequency list (if it is on it at all), and the direction of the swap.

## Why it exists

A learner of Ukrainian meets forms in real text that older materials quietly
taught as normal but that idiomatic Ukrainian replaces: russianisms, surzhyk, the
active-participle calque (dijuchyj rewritten as a relative clause), and other
loans. This thread makes that visible on the exact list the learner studies from,
and measures how often the flagged form is actually more common in the corpus
than its native replacement.

## Source and license

- **Source:** LanguageTool's Ukrainian replacement tables, maintained by the
  brown-uk group (Andriy Rysin and contributors), the open Pravopysnyk-style
  layer of the same project that publishes the VESUM morphological dictionary.
  Two files:
  - `replace.txt` (the standard replace table, treated here as the **hard** tier),
  - `replace_soft.txt` (soft suggestions, the **soft** tier).
  Located under
  `languagetool-language-modules/uk/src/main/resources/org/languagetool/rules/uk/`.
- **License:** LanguageTool's Ukrainian resources are **LGPL-2.1**. We
  redistribute only the factual flagged-form-to-suggestion assignment for the
  lemmas already on our list (never the tooling's rule XML or code), attribute the
  source, and carry the LGPL notice on the derived download.

## Honesty limits (all surfaced on the page)

- **Unvalidated and prescriptive.** This is what an open style checker would
  replace, an editorial opinion, not a settled linguistic verdict. Some entries
  (policeyskyj -> policijnyj, bezkoshtovnyj -> bezplatnyj) are live usage debates.
- **Broader than strictly-Russian.** The core is derussification (russianisms,
  surzhyk, calques), but the tooling's scope also covers other non-idiomatic
  loans and forms. We do not per-word label each as "russianism vs calque vs
  loan"; that taxonomy is not in the source and we do not invent it.
- **Proper nouns dropped.** PROPN rows (orthographic / transliteration fixes like
  Bilorusiya -> Bilorus) are excluded: they are not the "learn the idiomatic word"
  target.
- **Lemma-string join.** Matched to our list by exact lemma string plus a
  part-of-speech sanity filter; a handful of entries may be wordforms.
- **First suggestion is the tooling's own order.** The primary suggestion shown is
  the tooling's first listed alternative, kept verbatim; the full alternative list
  ships in the JSON.

## Native-speaker review (the overlay)

The tooling table above stays unvalidated. Beside it,
`derussification-reviews.json` holds the verdicts of paid native-speaker
reviewers from the Validation Desk (`/validate`, `docs/surfaces/validate.md`):
for a flagged lemma, whether a learner should avoid it and whether the
replacement is right, in the reviewer's own words, each accepted by the owner
before it counts. It is generated by `npm run validate:export` (never edited by
hand) and never rewrites this table, so a re-extraction cannot clobber it; a
review whose lemma drops out of the table on re-extraction is skipped by the
export. Reviewers are named only on their opt-in.

## Reproduce

Not run in the build or CI (it needs the LanguageTool resource files, which are
not vendored). To refresh:

```bash
base=languagetool-language-modules/uk/src/main/resources/org/languagetool/rules/uk
git clone --depth 1 https://github.com/languagetool-org/languagetool.git /tmp/lt
python3 scripts/ukrainian-frequency/extract-derussification.py \
  --replace /tmp/lt/$base/replace.txt \
  --replace-soft /tmp/lt/$base/replace_soft.txt
```

Then commit the regenerated artifacts. CI re-verifies them against each other and
against the seed (`__tests__/derussification.test.js`), not against the clone.
