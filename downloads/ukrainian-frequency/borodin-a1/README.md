# 1000 i 1 slovo (A1) joined onto the Ukrainian frequency list

The "1000 i 1 slovo" A1 lexical minimum for Ukrainian as a foreign
language (Kseniia Borodin and Oksana Turkevych; Slavic Language Education 3,
LANGUAGE-LAB, Humboldt-Universitat zu Berlin; https://doi.org/10.18452/28236),
joined onto the uavocab.org/research top-12,000
lemma frequency list (UberText 2.0, lang-uk).

One row per published entry, verbatim: the entry text, whether it is a word
or phrase entry, and, where a headword matches a lemma on the frequency
list, the frequency rank, lemma, POS, and the validated PULS CEFR level
where one exists (Synchak et al. 2025, by permission).

The minimum carries no part of speech, so the join is by spelling: `rank`,
`lemma` and `pos` are the spelling's most frequent reading on the list, and
`puls_level` is the EASIEST level the profile gives any reading of that
spelling. The two can belong to different readings: багато is listed at rank
280 as a determiner (validated A2) with puls_level A1, the level of the
adverb at rank 542. For a per-reading level, join `rank` to
validated-cefr/lemmas.csv instead.

Where it fits: this is the syllabus side of the A1 comparison. The research
page reads it for the two-source consensus (how many of the joined lemmas
the validated profile also puts at A1) and for the teachability trade (the
share of running text the matched lemmas cover, against a same-size
frequency core). ../minimums-crosswalk/ classifies every disagreement
between this volume, the A2 volume and the whole profile; the text profiler
offers it as a known-word set; the ukvocab Python package ships it; and its
multiword entries are the phrase rows of ../b1-extension/multiword.csv. The
sibling volume is ../borodin-a2/.
